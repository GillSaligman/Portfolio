About

I'm a mechanical engineer who ended up doing robotics because I like every part of it: the mechanism, the electronics, the code, and especially the moment a machine finally works.

I do my best work on hard problems with no manual, like old machines nobody has documentation for, and I'd rather understand a system than work around it. Right now I'm building a CNC mill at home focused on rigidity and closed-loop control.

Design

SolidWorks, Fusion 360, GD&T, DFM, FEA

Fabrication

CNC milling, lathe, EDM, laser cutting, 3D printing, sheet metal

Electronics & code
